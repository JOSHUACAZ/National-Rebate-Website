Network logic: BrandSource uses the Café National program. Nationwide/NMG uses the Nationwide Café combined program instead; the Café National rebate is intentionally excluded from the Nationwide path.
Profile Laundry: The official riser/pedestal rebate says it cannot be combined with any other GE Appliances rebate or promotion. The calculator therefore shows its value, but the headline total chooses the better of that rebate or the other compatible rebates rather than stacking it on top.
Printing: “Print Eligible Rebate Forms” creates one PDF packet containing official forms that show an eligible payout. If an exclusive alternative rebate is eligible, the print packet includes it as an available option even though it is not double-counted in the headline total.
Privacy: Appliance model numbers stay in this browser and are not sent to analytics. Anonymous usage metrics such as page visits, selected dealer network, package size, rebate counts, total calculated rebate amount, qualifying rebate programs, and print actions may be collected to understand site usage.
